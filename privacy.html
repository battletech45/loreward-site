Privacy Policy — Loreward Uploader

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Loreward Uploader is a personal tool used by the operator of the HFY Loreward YouTube channel to publish the channel's own original videos.

Data accessed

With the operator's authorization, the tool uses YouTube API Services via OAuth 2.0 to upload videos and thumbnails, upload captions, and set basic video metadata. OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret, refresh token) are stored on the operator's private server and are never shared or sold.

How data is used

Solely to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own channel. No analytics, no advertising use, no third-party sharing.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the stored credentials.

Agreements

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Contact the channel owner via the HFY Loreward channel page.
