Loreward Uploader

The publishing tool behind HFY Loreward.

Loreward Uploader is a personal tool operated by the owner of the HFY Loreward YouTube channel. It narrates, renders, and uploads the channel's own original humanity-first science fiction videos — each story written for the channel, reviewed before publication, and narrated with AI text-to-speech over atmospheric visuals with word-synced captions.
